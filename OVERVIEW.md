Sets up Manukora AgentOS on your Mac, then keeps it up to date.

The plugin adds an AgentOS setup screen to the sidebar. It checks your Mac, installs what is missing for your user, creates your AgentOS folder, starts onboarding, and walks you through connecting your accounts and tools. It also lets you report problems to AI & Systems and install updates.

It runs commands on your Mac and writes to your AgentOS folder. Sign-ins and tokens are entered by you, and tokens are never shown or logged.

For Manukora staff. Requires Paseo 0.15.3 or later and macOS.
